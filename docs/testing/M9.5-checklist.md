# M9.5 checklist: the website on a phone

Playwright covers the done-when at 390 px (`apps/web/e2e/office.spec.ts`, "on a phone: a tech
starts a job…"). These steps need a real phone, because they use the camera and microphone.

Open the site on the phone, sign in as the demo tech, and open today's job.

1. **Start Job.** The status changes to In progress and the button becomes Move Back to Scheduled.
2. **Checklist.** Tap Leaking on an item; a Where field appears. Type a place, tap elsewhere,
   reload: it is still there.
3. **Test.** Add Test → Pressure test. While typing the pressures the result appears
   ("Dropped 8 PSI in 15 min"). Add Test.
4. **Finding.** Add Finding, pick a severity, tap the pool diagram to drop the pin, Add Finding.
   The pin shows on the diagram with its number.
5. **Photo.** Add Photo opens the camera. Take a photo. It appears under Photos and Recordings.
6. **Mark Up.** Tap Mark Up on the photo, drag a circle, Save. The circle shows on the photo.
7. **Video.** Add Video, record a few seconds. It plays in the gallery.
8. **Voice note.** Record Voice Note, allow the microphone, say a sentence, Stop and Save. It
   plays in the gallery. With `DEEPGRAM_API_KEY` set, the transcript appears after a reload.
9. **Signature.** Add Signature, have someone sign with a finger, Save Signature. The page does
   not scroll while signing.
10. **Quote.** Start Quote, add an item from the price book and a custom line, change a quantity,
    Send Quote, copy the customer link and open it.
11. Nothing on any of these screens scrolls sideways.
