# M10.5 checklist: routes and scheduling

Playwright covers the done-when (`apps/web/e2e/routes.spec.ts`: a badly ordered day is planned
and keeps its promised time, a job moves from the tray with Move… and back by dragging, a
suggested time schedules it, and addresses on the job page and Today link to directions). CI
has no Mapbox key, so drive times there are the built-in estimate. These steps need the real key and real phones.

## Setup (owner, once)
1. Mapbox account (with billing details) → Tokens → Create a token with the default scopes
   and **no URL restrictions** (the calls come from our server, not a browser). It is used
   for the Geocoding API (v6, permanent) and the Matrix API. Put it in Vercel as
   `MAPBOX_TOKEN` (Sensitive). Never in the app, chat, or the repo.
   Optional: `ROUTING_DAILY_LIMIT` (calls per shop per day, default 300).
2. Check Mapbox's price for **permanent** geocoding (we store coordinates) at mapbox.com/pricing.
3. Apply the database migrations: `pnpm db:deploy`.
4. Settings › Scheduling: Find From Address for the shop (or Use My Location at the shop), then
   Find Pool Locations for pools added before the key was set.

## Check
1. **Real drive times:** Schedule → a day with jobs → Plan Route. The message names the total
   driving; it should be close to what Google or Apple Maps says for the same stops.
2. **Promised time:** on a job, tick "The customer was told this time", save, and Plan Route
   that day: the job keeps its time and shows "Fixed time".
3. **Drag on a computer:** drag a job by its grip to another tech, another place in the same
   route, and back to Needs a Time. Week view: drag a job to another day.
4. **Phone (390 px):** Schedule day view → Move… on a job → another tech and day → Move. Drag
   by the grip with a finger too.
5. **Suggestions:** a job in Needs a Time → Suggest Times: the first suggestion should be a tech
   already working near that pool. Schedule it and check the day it lands in.
6. **New Job:** existing customer and pool → Suggest Times → Use fills in date, time, and tech.
7. **Address links:** on an iPhone, tapping the job page's address opens Apple Maps with
   directions; on Android it opens Google Maps. In the phone app, the job screen's address
   opens the phone's maps app.
8. **Today (as a tech):** the route in order with drive times; tapping an address opens
   directions; On My Way opens Messages to the customer with "I'm on my way and should be there about …".
9. **Mark Location Here:** for a pool that is "Not on the map yet", at the pool on the job page
   → Mark Location Here; the line goes away and its address link points to that spot.
10. **No key:** with `MAPBOX_TOKEN` removed, everything above still works with estimated drive
    times, and Settings › Scheduling says so.
