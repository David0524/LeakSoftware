# Copy cuts: fixing the machine-written tells

Owner, 2026-10-26: "Fix the tells." The app shouldn't read machine-written. Every change below is
on all four platforms (desktop and mobile website, iPhone, Android) with the same wording, unless
a line says otherwise.

## The rule

- **Say it once, and only if the design doesn't.** A sentence that explains how the screen works
  (what a number means, that something saves, what a button does) is cut when the layout, the
  label, or the control already shows it.
- **No description under a page title.** The settings list already says what each page is. A
  title keeps a line only when it carries a real consequence (Checklist Components: changes reach
  new jobs only; Sundeck Pro Billing: not what customers pay you).
- **A footer or hint stays only when the label alone could mislead, or when money, a message to a
  customer, or deletion is involved.** When one stays it is one short sentence (about 12 words
  or fewer), and the shapes vary: no run of "X can't be Y until Z" or "Techs add are recorded and
  shown on…".
- **A count shows only when someone can act on it.** A tab badge counts what needs attention, not
  every unanswered item. A summary line leaves out parts that are zero ("1 of 133 answered", not
  "1 of 133 answered · 0 need attention · 0 leaking").
- **Kept as they were:** legal text, error messages that say what to do, money amounts, and the
  copy App Review relies on (`docs/release/APP_STORE.md`).
- House rules still hold: Title Case for buttons, segments and titles; sentence case elsewhere;
  no em dashes in interface copy.

Shared strings live in `packages/core` with Swift copies in `apps/ios/Sources/SundeckCore`; the
iPhone fixtures in `apps/ios/Tests/Fixtures` were regenerated (`pnpm --filter @leakbook/ios
fixtures`). New core helpers: `needAttentionText`, `inspectionTallyText`, `checklistTallyText`
(`packages/core/src/checklist-summary.ts`, ported in `ChecklistSummary.swift`).

## Not copy, but in the same piece of work

- **Price Book Add/Edit Item**: Name and Price are now required. On the website they show
  "Required" instead of "Optional" (the grouped field shows "Optional" for any typed field not
  marked required) and the browser stops a blank save; the server and both phone apps already
  refused a blank one with "Enter a name." / "Enter a price…". The iPhone and Android forms now
  show "Required" in the two fields too. The same "Optional" bug was fixed on the other website
  forms whose server check rejects a blank: Checklist Components (Question, Name), Test Types
  (Name and the built-in tests' pass and fail lines), Severities (Name), Exclusion Reasons
  (Reason), Report Templates (Name), Scheduling and Routes (usual job length, maintenance visit
  minutes, weeks ahead). Fields that may be blank (quote and invoice reminder days, the payments
  percentages, the pool's city, state and ZIP) stay optional.
- **Schedule, whose schedule chips**: the Reorder Techs button used to sit beside the sideways
  scrolling row of chips, so the viewport edge cut the last name in half ("Luis Ramire"). The
  button is now the last item inside the scrolling row, so the row runs edge to edge and every
  name is whole when you scroll to it (pills also never truncate their text on the iPhone).
  Website, iPhone and Android.

## Left alone on purpose

Customer-facing report, invoice and quote pages (another piece of work); the staff admin portal;
legal and consent text; error messages that say what to do; the pool diagram's gesture help; the
Settings list's one-line descriptions; form examples such as "Gate code, dog, where to park.";
"Blank: route planning picks it."; the card fee's state-law caution; the tax-rate and
maintenance-price notes that say what a customer will be charged; and the notices that tell a
tech what to do next.
