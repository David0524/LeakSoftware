# Team colors, Week with routes, plan visits that keep their day, and the demo

Owner, 2026-10-20 (after TestFlight 1012/1013). Built on all four platforms (desktop and phone
website, iPhone, Android) with the same words and behavior.

## 1. Today's card: a swipe is not a tap; the address isn't a link

"It's happening because I'm tapping the name while I swipe. … just recognize the swipe as
different from a tap. The address should no longer be clickable on this [tile]."

- The name opens the job only on a clean tap: the finger lifts without moving more than about
  10 pt. Once a sideways drag starts, no button on the card acts on the lift. The iPhone does
  this as of 1012; the website suppresses the click after a drag; Android must do the same,
  either by cancelling the press when the pan activates or by guarding it the way the iPhone
  does.
- The address on the card is plain text, not a link. Directions is the round button. The pin
  symbol and the not-on-map warning stay.

## 2. Team: one list, profiles and switches together

"Mix the tech profiles with the role/on and off switch. Put the active techs on top -
alphabetical and the inactive beneath them - in order of departure."

- **The list:** Settings › Team, the same screen on every platform, is one inset grouped list.
  - **Active:** every active member, A to Z by name.
  - **Turned off:** below it, ordered by when each was turned off, the most recent first.
- **Each row:**
  - the tech's color dot, their name in 17 pt, and their role under it in 15 pt;
  - at the right, the On/Off switch (owners and admins only; disabled for yourself);
  - tapping the row opens the profile: contact details, emergency contact, notes, **Role**,
    **Color**, and the switch again;
  - turning someone off asks first: "Turn off <Name>? They can't sign in, and their coming jobs
    stay as they are."
- **Data:**
  - New `membership.disabled_at` (timestamptz), set by a trigger when `status` becomes
    `disabled` and cleared when it becomes `active`.
  - The order is core `teamOrder(members)`.
  - Invites still waiting stay in their own section under the list.

## 3. A color for every tech

"Let's assign colors to each tech. Maybe just a dot beside their name or a tint on their card.
Something so the owner can easily converse with them."

- **The palette:** ten colors in `brand/tokens.json` › `color.tech`, each with light and dark
  values that keep at least 3:1 against the surface, so a dot is visible:
  - Light: blue `#1F6FAE`, green `#2E8B57`, orange `#D9822B`, purple `#7B5CC4`, pink `#C2477E`,
    teal `#15899A`, red `#C8463D`, olive `#7A8B2E`, brown `#9A6B3F`, slate `#5A6875`.
  - Dark: the same hues, lifted for dark mode, for example blue `#4A93D0`.
  - Names are shown to the owner in the color picker: Blue, Green, Orange, Purple, Pink, Teal,
    Red, Olive, Brown, Slate.
- **Assignment:**
  - New `membership.color` (text, one of the palette keys; owners and admins can change it).
  - A new member gets the first palette color no active member uses, in palette order (core
    `nextTechColor`).
  - Existing members get colors by join order in the migration.
- **Where it shows:**
  - **A 10 pt dot before the tech's name everywhere a tech is named:**
    - Whose Day;
    - the Tech row on a job;
    - Schedule's tech headers and Week rows;
    - Plan Week's tech list;
    - the Move sheet's tech picker;
    - Team.
  - **Schedule and Week cards** get a 4 pt stripe on their leading edge in their tech's color.
  - **Map pins** stay in their state colors (to do, current, done), so the maps keep their
    meaning.
  - Today's card stays water blue.
- **Accessibility:** a color is never the only cue. The tech's name or initials are always
  beside it, and VoiceOver and TalkBack read the name, not the color.

## 4. "Maps", not "Apple Maps" or "Google Maps"

"Change 'Google Maps' and 'Apple Maps' to just 'Maps'."

- **Buttons and links:** "Open in Maps", "Directions", "Next Stop in Maps" and "Open Route in
  Maps" never name the maps company.
- **The setting:** Settings › Directions open in keeps the two choices, since that's where
  someone picks one. Its options read "Maps (Apple)" and "Maps (Google)", and Automatic stays
  first.

## 5. Weekly maintenance visits keep their day; moving one asks

"Customers on weekly maintenance plans should be assigned the same day/tech each appointment
unless changed. When rearranging one of these, have a pop up ask 'change schedule' or 'just
today'."

- A plan's coming visits always fall on its weekday, with its tech; this is already how
  `service_plan` makes them. Nothing changes them except the plan, or a "Just This Visit".
- **Moving a plan visit** asks with an action sheet, whether by drag, Move…, a menu, or Plan
  Week's results (Plan Week itself still leaves plan visits in place):
  - Title: "Move <Customer>'s visits?"
  - **Change Schedule**: every coming visit moves to the new weekday and tech, through the plan
    (`setServiceDay`). The banner says "<Customer> is now on <Weekday>s with <Tech>", with Undo.
  - **Just This Visit**: only this visit moves. It's marked as moved
    (`job.plan_moved = true`), so remaking the plan's visits never puts it back, and next week
    is unchanged. The banner says "Moved this visit only", with Undo.
  - **Cancel**.
- On the website and in the apps the buttons read "Change Schedule" and "Just This Visit".
  ("Just today" in the owner's words; the visit may not be today.)
- **Data:** new `job.plan_moved boolean not null default false`. `app.service_plan_changed` and
  the visit-making job skip and keep visits with `plan_moved`. Core
  `planVisitMoveChoices(job)` returns whether to ask.

## 6. Routes goes into Week; the desktop sidebar

"On routes, you can't optimize or rearrange placement. It's actually a pretty useless tab.
Let's just build that all into Week." And: "On desktop, let's keep 'needs a time' on the left
side bar in 'routes' page. Or maybe they should always be on the right side? Research and make
the best call."

- **No Routes tab.** Schedule's views are Day, Week and Month, and the routes work moves into
  Week:
  - every tech's row for each day;
  - Optimize per day: the day's "⋯" menu, plus Optimize Week, which is Plan Week;
  - drag to rearrange within a day or move to another day or tech (plan visits ask, as in
    section 5);
  - **Customers to Add** (plans without a day) with Suggest Days;
  - **Needs a Time**.
- **Desktop and iPad width (≥ 1024 pt): a left sidebar.** It holds Needs a Time, then Customers
  to Add, each a collapsible section with its count, 280 pt wide, scrolling on its own, with
  the board to its right.
  - **Why left:** on Apple platforms the sidebar on the left is where you pick and drag from
    (Mail's mailboxes, Calendar's calendar list, Finder), and the right side is the inspector,
    for details of the selection. Dispatch boards (Jobber, Housecall Pro, ServiceTitan) also
    keep the unscheduled list on the left, beside the techs' names, so a drag is a short move
    right onto a row.
  - It also keeps the techs' names and colors next to the work being assigned.
- **Phones:** Needs a Time and Customers to Add stay as trays above the board, as now.

## 7. Smaller cards on desktop

"On desktop, the schedule cards are still way too big. It's still tough for a manager to set
everyone's routes."

- **Desktop (≥ 1024 pt) Day and Week cards are one line, 36 pt tall:**
  - the tech color stripe;
  - the stop number, the customer's name in 15 pt semibold, and the job type in 13 pt;
  - the lock and not-on-map symbols;
  - the grip on the right;
  - hovering shows the address and time in a tooltip, and a click opens the job.
- About 12 stops then fit on a day without scrolling.
- Drag still works anywhere on the card, the grip included.
- Phones keep their current cards.

## 8. Customers to Add: the whole tile drags

"You can't drag the 6 dots that are meant for dragging … you should be able to drag the entire
tile."

The whole tile is the drag handle, grip included, on every platform. A tap still opens it.

## 10. The demo company, rebuilt

"When you are done clean out all the test jobs. Demo should show the following: Dana and Jake
riding together on all leak detections. 2-3 detections per day. 50 weekly maintenance pools for
each Luis, Priya, and Tony. Scatter in 1 repairs per day for each of them."

- **`pnpm demo:reset`** rebuilds the demo company on the dev project through the Management
  API, and only that company. There's a dry run by default; `--apply` changes data.
  - **Removes:** every job, quote, invoice, report and plan, plus customers and pools that only
    test runs made: the e2e names with run suffixes, "Apple Map", "Plan Week" and similar.
    Rows are soft-deleted where the schema keeps history. Sent reports and original photos stay
    in storage, as the rules require.
  - **Keeps:** the company, its settings and the team.
- **Team:** Dana (owner), Jake, Luis, Priya and Tony, each with a color. Users who don't exist
  yet are made as demo users with the demo password, as the seed does for the others.
- **Leak detections:**
  - 2–3 a working day (Monday to Friday), from last Monday to two weeks ahead;
  - Dana is the tech and Jake the second tech on every one;
  - past ones are finished with reports, today's are a mix, and future ones are scheduled.
- **Maintenance:**
  - 50 customers each for Luis, Priya and Tony;
  - each has a weekly plan on a fixed weekday, 10 per weekday per tech;
  - each customer has one pool with real coordinates around the shop's city, so no lookups
    run.
- **Repairs:** 1 a working day for each of Luis, Priya and Tony, at one of their own customers'
  pools, over the same dates.
- **Addresses:** realistic street addresses with stored coordinates and `geocoded_address` set,
  so nothing is looked up or shown as not on the map.
- **Visits:** they come from the plans through the normal visit-making code, not inserted
  directly.
