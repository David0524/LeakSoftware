# Autopay, Card on File, and Pay Over Time

Owner's brief (2026-11-16): cards and bank accounts on file, autopay for monthly maintenance
invoices with retries, card-on-file requests, and financing on quotes and invoices (Triton has
all four). Same screens and words on the desktop website, the phone website, the iPhone app and
the Android app; the customer's pages (invoice, quote, Request Card on File) are web only, since
customers use the browser.

Payments stay as they are: Stripe Connect Standard accounts, direct charges, the shop is the
merchant of record. Every new charge and saved method lives on the shop's connected account.

## 1. A card or bank account on file

- **Stripe holds it.** Each Sundeck customer gets a Stripe Customer on the shop's account the
  first time they save something (`app.stripe_customer`, server-only). We keep only a summary:
  `customer_payment_method` (kind card or US bank account, brand, last 4, expiry, bank name,
  Stripe ids). One live method per customer; saving a new one replaces the old one, which is
  detached in Stripe too.
- **Who sees it:** owners and admins (read role `manager`; their phones sync it). Nobody writes
  it through the API; the webhook does. `customer.autopay` is server-written as well: the
  office's Turn Off and Turn On go through the server (`set_customer_autopay`), so a tech can't
  change it and every change is a manager's.
- **Two ways to save:**
  1. **Save for Autopay** on the invoice page: a switch above the Pay button, shown when the
     shop offers autopay and the customer doesn't pay automatically yet. Under it, what they
     agree to: "I authorize Blue Wave Pools to save this card or bank account with Stripe and
     charge it for my invoices when they’re sent, until I ask Blue Wave Pools to stop." (plus
     "Card payments include a 3% card fee; bank account payments have none." when the shop
     passes the fee on). On, Checkout offers card and bank account only (Affirm, Klarna and Link
     can't be charged later) with `setup_future_usage: off_session`; the webhook saves the
     method and turns autopay on.
  2. **Request Card on File** from the customer's page in the office: by email, by a text from
     the shop's number (when it has one), or (apps) a text from the person's own phone, as an
     invoice link is. The link (`/card/<token>`, 30 days, reused while a week is left) opens
     **Automatic Payments**: the shop's header, one line of what happens, **Save Card or Bank
     Account** (Stripe Checkout in setup mode, embedded when the publishable key is set), and
     the consent text. Nothing is charged. Back on the page: "Saved. Your invoices from … will
     be paid automatically, and you’ll get a receipt each time."

## 2. Autopay

- **When:** a monthly maintenance invoice (`invoice.period_month`) is sent, by hand, Send All, or
  on its invoice day, for a customer with autopay on and a method saved, in a shop with Autopay
  on. Every send goes through `sendInvoiceWithAutopay` (`apps/web/src/server/autopay.ts`). The
  invoice email then says "Amount due: $164.00, paid automatically with your Visa •••• 4242;
  there’s nothing to do." The charge runs right after the send, off-session, on the shop's
  account (`paymentIntents.create`, `confirm`, `off_session`, idempotency key per try).
- **Card fee:** applies to cards exactly as on Checkout (its own amount, kept off the invoice's
  payment); bank accounts have none. The consent text says so when saving.
- **Outcomes:** card succeeded: the payment is recorded, the receipt goes out, everyone who
  hears about payments hears "Jane Smith paid $164.00 (invoice 1042)". Bank: processing (a
  pending payment), settled by `payment_intent.succeeded` / `payment_failed`. Declined: no
  payment; `invoice.autopay_status = failed` with Stripe's reason and the next try.
- **Failed:** the first failure emails the customer their normal pay link ("We couldn’t charge
  your Visa •••• 4242 for invoice #1042 ($164.00). We’ll try again on Dec 4, or you can pay now
  with any card or bank account:"). Owners and admins get a push ("Autopay failed: Jane Smith’s
  $164.00 payment (invoice 1042) didn’t go through: Your card was declined.") on the first
  failure and when the last try fails, not every try.
- **Retries** (daily run, `autopay_retries_due`): per shop, **Retry Failed Payments** Off or 1 to
  3 more tries (default 2), **Try Again** every 1, 2, 3, 5 or 7 days (default 3). A retry runs
  only while the invoice is still owed and the customer still has autopay and a method.
- **Other invoices** are never charged by themselves: owners and admins tap **Charge Card on
  File** on the invoice. Confirmation: "Charge Visa •••• 4242?" / "Jane Smith is charged $168.92
  now. That includes the 3% card fee ($4.92)." with **Cancel** and **Charge $168.92**. It also
  works for a monthly invoice whose autopay failed, and for customers with autopay off (the
  card is saved; the office decides). No retries for these.

## 3. Pay Over Time (Affirm and Klarna)

- Offered in Checkout on quote deposits and invoices when all three hold: the shop's **Offer Pay
  Over Time** is on (default), the amount qualifies (Affirm $50 to $30,000, Klarna $50 to
  $10,000; we use $50 for Klarna too), and Stripe says the capability is `active` on the shop's
  account (`accounts.retrieve`, kept 10 minutes per server; a failed lookup offers neither and
  isn't kept). An inactive method is never sent, so Checkout never fails on it.
- Card fee off: one Checkout with every method plus Affirm and Klarna. Card fee on: a third
  button, **Pay $164.00 Over Time** (no fee; surcharging them isn't allowed), beside Pay by Bank
  and Pay by Card.
- The customer's pages say "Pay over time with Affirm or Klarna" (or just the one that's on)
  under the pay button.
- **Settings › Payments and Deposits** shows the status under the switch: "Affirm and Klarna are
  on in your Stripe account." or how to turn them on: "In your Stripe Dashboard, open Settings ›
  Payment methods and turn on Affirm and Klarna; they show here once Stripe approves them."

**Autopay group** (inset grouped list, after Contact): **Status** On/Off; the method ("Visa ••••
4242", "Expires 04/30") or "No card or bank account on file"; **Turn Off Autopay** (alert:
"Turn off autopay?" / "Invoices are no longer charged automatically. The card stays saved for
Charge Card on File."), or **Turn On Autopay** when saved but off; **Request Card on File**;
**Remove Card** (red; "Remove the saved card?"). Footer explains what happens now. Apps call
`POST /api/customers/{id}/autopay` and `POST /api/invoices/{id}/charge`; the rows then sync.

**Invoice line:** "Paid automatically with Visa •••• 4242", "Charged to Chase •••• 6789; the bank
payment is processing", "Autopay failed: Your card was declined. Trying again Dec 4." (red). The
customer's invoice page: "Paid automatically with Visa •••• 4242." or "Autopay failed: we
couldn’t charge Visa •••• 4242. Please pay below."

Wording lives in `packages/core/src/autopay.ts` and its Swift twin
`apps/ios/Sources/SundeckCore/Autopay.swift` (fixture-checked).

## Data (migration `20261022004400_autopay.sql`)

- `customer.autopay`, `invoice.autopay_status / autopay_method / autopay_failure /
  autopay_tries / autopay_next_try / autopay_at` (server-written).
- `customer_payment_method` (synced, managers read, server writes), `app.stripe_customer`,
  `app.card_request`.
- Service-role functions: `autopay_details` (also managers, for Charge Card on File),
  `autopay_record`, `autopay_settle`, `autopay_retries_due`, `autopay_save_method`,
  `claim_intent_notification`, `card_request_details`, `stripe_customer_for`,
  `stripe_save_customer`. Managers: `set_customer_autopay`, `remove_payment_method`,
  `card_request_link`. Anyone with an invoice link: `invoice_autopay_for_token`.
- Webhook: the existing route; new handling for `checkout.session.completed` (setup mode and
  Save for Autopay) and `payment_intent.succeeded` / `payment_intent.payment_failed` with
  `metadata.kind = autopay`. **The shop's Connect webhook endpoint must send
  `payment_intent.succeeded` and `payment_intent.payment_failed`** (bank autopay settles
  through them).

## Decisions for the owner

1. **Autopay is on for every shop by default** (it does nothing until a customer saves a card
   with consent). Offer Pay Over Time is on by default too, and does nothing until Stripe
   approves Affirm or Klarna.
2. **Only monthly maintenance invoices are charged automatically.** One-off invoices need Charge
   Card on File. Quote deposits are never charged from a saved card.
3. **Customer email on the first failure only; owners and admins on the first and the last.**
4. **Affirm and Klarna payments are recorded as card payments** on the invoice ("Card"), since
   the payment table knows card, bank, cash, check and other.
5. **The customer can't turn autopay off themselves** (no customer portal); the consent text
   tells them to ask the shop, and the office's Turn Off is one tap.
6. Klarna's minimum is set to $50 like Affirm's, to keep one rule; Stripe allows lower.

## Not now

- A customer portal to manage saved cards; several cards per customer; autopay for quote
  deposits; charging cards before an invoice is sent.
