# First win and the start of the day (owner, 2026-10-22)

> "First win within about 60 seconds: pre-fill one sample job for the day (marked as a
> sample), open it to a finished report with their own logo, then prompt 'Send yourself this
> report'."
>
> "Techs: the first stop of the day opens in one tap from the notification."

Every platform: the desktop and mobile website, the iPhone app, the Android app.

## 1. The sample job

**Made by the database, once.** `create_company` calls `app.create_sample_job` for Leak
Detection and Both shops (migration `20261022000400_sample_job.sql`). It syncs to the phones
like any job, so all four platforms get it with no app code making it. A shop gets one, ever:
a deleted sample never comes back. If making it fails, the shop is still created (a warning
only). Pool-only shops get none, since the sample is a leak detection report.

**Config, not code.** The made-up customer ("Sample Customer", 123 Sample Way), verdicts, tests,
findings and report text are the `sample_job` row of `app.config_default`. The checklist is the
shop's own leak checklist.

**What it is.** A detection job on today's schedule at 9:00 (shop time) for the owner, status
Report Sent, no job number (the first real job is still #1001), its checklist finished: the
return line leaking (pressure test fail, dye test fail, bucket test fail), the light niche
needing attention, the vinyl items not applicable, the rest not leaking; two findings. Its
report (homeowner, version 1) is finished with a link, "not signed: sample report", and its
snapshot carries `job.sample`.

**Honest.** The report's first sentence says it's a sample, and the report page and PDF show a
banner: "Sample report. The customer, pool, and findings are made up to show what your
customers receive."

**Their own logo.** The sample's report is the one finished report allowed to change: when the
shop's name, logo, colors, contact details or disclaimer change in Settings, a trigger copies
the new company block into its snapshot (`app.sample_report_follows_shop`). Its PDF is drawn
fresh each time instead of stored. Its text never changes (`freeze_sent_report` still refuses).

**Marked "Sample" everywhere it shows.** A Sample pill (status warning color, white text, like
the other status chips) on its Today row, Schedule card, Jobs row (and "Sample job" where the
number would be) and its page, on every platform.

**Never counted.** `job.sample` is server-only (no client grant). `app.record_activity` skips
sample jobs, so no Activity and no weekly-summary "jobs done"; `record_report_opened` never
reports it opened (no push when the owner opens their own email); the Dashboard's job counts
leave it out; it has no fee, quote, invoice or payment. Getting Started doesn't count its job,
customer or report. Its made-up address counts as already looked up, so no paid map lookup is
spent on it.

## 2. Opening it

The sample's page is its finished report under one card:

- **Sample Job**: what it is, and (no logo yet) "Add your logo in Settings and it shows on this
  report" with **Add Your Logo**.
- **Send Yourself This Report** (primary, filled blue): the report's email to the signed-in
  person's own address, taken from their sign-in, never from the request, with no CC
  (`POST /api/jobs/<id>/sample`, the website's `sendSampleAction`; both reuse `sendReport`).
  The email says "Here's your sample leak detection report, the way your customers get it."
  Under the buttons: "It goes to you@shop.com, and only you." When the server has no email,
  the report opens instead.
- **View Report** (tinted): the customer's page.
- **Delete Sample**: an action sheet ("Delete the sample job?" / "Its report and made-up customer
  go too. It won't come back.") then `delete_sample_job`, back to Today.

Then the report itself: on the website the customer's report view; in the apps a branded header
(logo, shop name, report title), the sample banner and the report's sections, with View Report
for the full page.

**Getting Started** gets a second step, right after Account created: "Send yourself the sample
report" ("See what your customers get, with your shop's name and logo."), which opens the
sample; done once it's emailed to yourself, or deleted. The onboarding's last page leads with
**See Your Sample Report** when the shop has one.

## 3. The start of the day

There was no morning push. The Live Activity (iPhone) and the ongoing notification (Android)
already open their stop's job when tapped, but the app starts them itself, so they only appear
once the app is opened during the work day. A server push would need a per-time-zone cron (the
site has one daily cron) and push keys.

So each phone schedules **one local notification for itself** at the start of the shop's working
day (Settings › Scheduling) on the next work day with stops: **"5 stops today"** /
**"First: Smith, 123 Palm Ln"** (core `dayStartAlert`, ported to Swift). It comes with the app
closed and with no signal, and is replaced whenever the person's stops change (each sync and
each minute while the app is open). The sample job is left out.

**One tap** opens Today with the first stop's job on top; if that job is gone from the phone
(deleted), Today alone. Its own switch: More › Notifications › **Start of the day** (on by
default, all four platforms, `notification_prefs.day`).
