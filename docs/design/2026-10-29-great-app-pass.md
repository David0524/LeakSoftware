# Great-app pass (2026-10-29)

Owner: "Do another pass through of the app. Check design, usability, etc." Then: "Do it regarding
what makes this a great app … Assume we are already good to be accepted by Apple."

Looked at every iPhone screen from CI (build 1068, light mode) and the website at desktop and
phone size, asking one question: does this make a tech's day or an owner's office work faster,
clearer or nicer?

## Fixed now (all four platforms)

- **Signing never dead-ends.** "13 checklist items are still to do" now has **Go to Checklist**
  under it, which opens the job's Checklist. Before, the tech had to find their way back.
- **Save Readings, not Submit.** The Chemical page's button says what it does; it confirms
  "Saved to the log."
- **Job page says each thing once.** The detection fee (or price) showed twice on Details (read
  only under Assigned to, editable under On site), and the tech showed twice (Assigned to and
  Schedule). Each now appears once; Change Schedule still sets the tech.
- **Jobs list lines never start with "·".** "Fri, Oct 30, 8:42 AM · Unassigned" wrapped as
  "· Unassigned" on its own line; the dot now stays with the time.
- **Reopen Job, not Start Job, after the report is sent.** A finished job's main blue button
  said Start Job; it now says Reopen Job, as a secondary button.
- **Website on phones:** Invoices pills read "All 26" (were "All26"), the Invoices search field
  no longer shrinks to a sliver beside Sort, and long settings values end in "…" instead of being
  cut mid-word.

## Recommended next (owner's call)

1. **New Job in 20 seconds.** It asks about 20 things. Show what a call needs (who, phone,
   address, job, when) and fold the rest (pool type, spa, gallons, shape, access notes, other
   emails) under "More Details". New Job is the office's most frequent task.
2. **An empty Today shows what's next.** "Nothing on your route today" is the first screen on a
   day off or before planning. Show the next working day instead ("Tomorrow: 4 stops, first at
   8:00 AM", tap to open it).
3. **Edit Finding shows the pool shape only until it's set.** Today every finding repeats seven
   shape tiles and Has a Spa above the pin, and tapping the chosen tile clears the shape. Once a
   pool has a shape, leave it to Edit Pool Shape on Findings.
4. **The forward step leads the job page.** An in-progress job's top button is Move Back to
   Scheduled, a step backward that's rarely wanted. Move it into a ••• menu and keep the
   forward step in the Next Step bar.
5. **Customers filters like Jobs.** Jobs sorts and filters from small toolbar buttons; Customers
   puts two full-width pickers above the list. Same pattern on both.

## Kept on purpose

The Today card (one tap to Start, Text, Call, Directions), the checklist's one-row answers with
Mark Remaining Not Leaking, the computed pressure-test line ("Dropped 8 PSI in 15 min"), Wrap-Up
as a numbered finish list, report links that show "Opened 2 times", and the Live Activity.
