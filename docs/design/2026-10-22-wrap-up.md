# Wrap-Up: the finish list

Owner, 2026-10-22, asked whether Wrap-Up could use a redesign; of three sketches
(`2026-10-22-wrap-up-options.png`) chose **A, the finish list**: "Let's go with A".

Replaces the Wrap-Up tab's four stacked sections (Report and Sign-Off, Signatures, Quotes,
Invoice), which on a phone ran about 15 screens with every editor open. Same layout, wording and
behavior on the desktop and phone website, iPhone and Android. Jobs without a Wrap-Up
(maintenance visits) are unchanged.

## The page

- **Subtitle under the tab's content title:** the customer and the count, "Maria Alvarez · 2 of 4
  done" ("All done" when every step is).
- **One inset grouped list (`.card`), a row per step, in this order:**
  1. **Report**: the homeowner report.
  2. **Quote**: one row per quote on the job, newest last. Repair jobs have no quote rows (a
     repair job is the quote's work), as now.
  3. **Customer signs**: Review with Customer and the signatures.
  4. **Invoice**: one row per invoice.
- **Each row:** a 30 pt round marker (green check when done; the step number in blue for the next
  step; the number in a gray ring for later steps), the step's title ("Report", "Quote #1006",
  "Customer signs", "Invoice #1025"), a secondary line with its state in words, the amount on the
  right for quotes and invoices (tabular digits), and a chevron. The whole row is one 44 pt+ target
  that opens the step.
- **Under the list, a plain link row** "New Quote" when a new quote can be started (no draft or
  accepted quote, not a repair job). Never "No quote yet" above a quote.
- **One primary button at the bottom** (filled blue, full width; on phones it sits above the tab
  bar and stays in view) naming the next step's action. Hidden when everything is done.

## States (shared rule in `packages/core`, ported to Swift with fixtures)

| Step | Secondary line | Done when | Next-step button |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Report | "Not written yet" · "Draft ready · 2 findings, 5 tests" · "Sent · version 2" | a draft exists or it was sent | Write the Draft |
| Quote | "Draft · not sent" · "Sent · waiting" · "Accepted · deposit paid" / "Accepted" · "Declined" · "Expired" | sent, accepted, declined or expired | Send Quote |
| Customer signs | "Not yet" · "Signed by Maria Alvarez" · "Not signed: customer not home" | signed, or skipped with a reason | Review with Customer |
| Invoice | "Draft · not sent" · "Sent · $649 due" · "Partly paid · $375 due" · "Paid" | sent or paid | Send Invoice |

- **The next step** is the first step in order that isn't done. Its marker is blue and the
  button names its action. Quote rows count as one step for the "N of M" count and the order (all
  of the job's quotes done = done); a job with no quotes counts no quote step.
- Customer signs can't be next before the report has a draft (its row reads "Write the report
  first" and opens Report).

## Opening a step

- A row opens that step's editor **in a sheet** (iPhone: `.sheet` with a NavigationStack and a
  large title and Done; Android: the app's sheet; website: the existing sheet on phones, a
  centered dialog on desktop), holding exactly today's section for it: Report (Write Draft,
  Report Text, versions, Send), the quote's card and builder, the signatures and Review with
  Customer, the invoice's card and editor. No editor shows on the list page itself.
- The bottom button does the same as tapping the next step's row, except Review with Customer,
  which starts the review flow directly, as today.
- Closing a sheet returns to the list, which reflects the change at once (local-first on the
  phones; optimistic on the website).

## Wording

Title Case for buttons and the step titles that are names ("Customer signs" is a step, sentence
case like a list row). No explanatory paragraphs on the list page: the secondary lines say what a
tech needs. VoiceOver/TalkBack read each row as "Step 3 of 4, Customer signs, not yet, next".
