# Wrap-Up: the finish list

Owner, 2026-10-22, asked whether Wrap-Up could use a redesign; of three sketches
(`2026-10-22-wrap-up-options.png`) chose **A, the finish list**: "Let's go with A".

Replaces the Wrap-Up tab's four stacked sections (Report and Sign-Off, Signatures, Quotes,
Invoice), which on a phone ran about 15 screens with every editor open. Same layout, wording and
behavior on the desktop and phone website, iPhone and Android. Jobs without a Wrap-Up
(maintenance visits) are unchanged.

## The page

- **Subtitle under the tab's content title:** the customer and the count, "Maria Alvarez · 2 of 4
  done" ("All done" when every step is).
- **One inset grouped list (`.card`), a row per step, in this order:**
  1. **Report**: the homeowner report.
  2. **Quote**: one row per quote on the job, newest last. Repair jobs have no quote rows (a
     repair job is the quote's work), as now.
  3. **Customer signs**: Review with Customer and the signatures.
  4. **Invoice**: one row per invoice.
- **Each row:** a 30 pt round marker (green check when done; the step number in blue for the next
  step; the number in a gray ring for later steps), the step's title ("Report", "Quote #1006",
  "Customer signs", "Invoice #1025"), a secondary line with its state in words, the amount on the
  right for quotes and invoices (tabular digits), and a chevron. The whole row is one 44 pt+ target
  that opens the step.
- **Under the list, a plain link row** "New Quote" when a new quote can be started (no draft or
  accepted quote, not a repair job). Never "No quote yet" above a quote.
- **One primary button at the bottom** (filled blue, full width; on phones it sits above the tab
  bar and stays in view) naming the next step's action. Hidden when everything is done.

## States (shared rule in `packages/core`, ported to Swift with fixtures)

| Step | Secondary line | Done when | Next-step button |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Report | "Not written yet" · "Draft ready · 2 findings, 5 tests" · "Sent · version 2" | a draft exists or it was sent | Write the Draft |
| Quote | "Draft · not sent" · "Sent · waiting" · "Accepted · deposit paid" / "Accepted" · "Declined" · "Expired" | sent, accepted, declined or expired | Send Quote |
| Customer signs | "Not yet" · "Signed by Maria Alvarez" · "Not signed: customer not home" | signed, or skipped with a reason | Review with Customer |
| Invoice | "Draft · not sent" · "Sent · $649 due" · "Partly paid · $375 due" · "Paid" | sent or paid | Send Invoice |

- **The next step** is the first step in order that isn't done. Its marker is blue and the
  button names its action. Quote rows count as one step for the "N of M" count and the order (all
  of the job's quotes done = done); a job with no quotes counts no quote step.
- Customer signs can't be next before the report has a draft (its row reads "Write the report
  first" and opens Report).

## Opening a step

- A row opens that step's editor **in a sheet** (iPhone: `.sheet` with a NavigationStack and a
  large title and Done; Android: the app's sheet; website: the existing sheet on phones and
  tablets; on a computer, 1024 px and wider, a panel beside the list instead, see "Desktop job
  page"), holding exactly today's section for it: Report (Write Draft,
  Report Text, versions, Send), the quote's card and builder, the signatures and Review with
  Customer, the invoice's card and editor. No editor shows on the list page itself.
- The bottom button does the same as tapping the next step's row, except Review with Customer,
  which starts the review flow directly, as today.
- Closing a sheet returns to the list, which reflects the change at once (local-first on the
  phones; optimistic on the website).

## Wording

Title Case for buttons and the step titles that are names ("Customer signs" is a step, sentence
case like a list row). No explanatory paragraphs on the list page: the secondary lines say what a
tech needs. VoiceOver/TalkBack read each row as "Step 3 of 4, Customer signs, not yet, next".

## Decisions made while building (for the owner)

- **Invoice row before there is one:** one "Invoice" row reading "Not started", so the list
  always has its last step; Send Invoice opens it with Create Invoice.
- **Sent without a signature:** a report finished with Send Without a Signature (no reason
  given) marks Customer signs done as "Sent without a signature", since nothing is left to sign.
- **Which signatures count:** the report's sign-off (inspection acknowledgement, completion);
  work authorization (signed before the work) and quote acceptance (signed on the quote) don't.
- **Report turned off in settings:** Report and Customer signs leave the list.
- **Start Job first:** on a job not started yet, the phone's bar still offers Start Job; the
  Wrap-Up button takes over once it's started.
- **Money:** amounts read as everywhere else in the app, with cents ("$649.00").
- **Write the Draft** opens the Report step (as its row does), per this spec; it no longer
  writes the draft straight from the bar.

## Maintenance visit report bar

Owner, 2026-10-22: "For maintenance plans, the visit report is really buried. I'd prefer not to
make a new tab for it since it's so simple for this. Maybe a floating bottom bar like in your wrap
up mock." Shared rule `visitReportBar` in `packages/core` (ported to Swift with fixtures).

- Maintenance jobs use the Next Step bar. Before the visit is done it keeps Complete Job on the
  Checklist as now; in an Automatic shop a line above it says "Sends by itself when you finish".
- Once done, on every tab, the bar is about the visit report: a status line and one button.
  - Sent: "Visit report sent · 2:14 PM" (a date when not today) and **View Report**, which opens
    the report.
  - Manual shop, not sent: "Ready to send" and **Send Visit Report**.
  - Automatic shop, not sent yet (no signal, or it failed): "Not sent yet" and Send Visit Report.
- Tapping the status line, or Send Visit Report, opens the Visit Report sheet: today's panel
  (send by email or text, Send Again, the versions).
- Computers show the same bar floating at the bottom of the job page once the visit is done
  (before that, the header and the checklist already have Complete Job).
- The Visit Report section stays in Details only until the visit is done, so a report can still
  be sent early; after that it lives on the bar.

## Desktop job page

Owner, 2026-10-22: "The browser's job page need different tabs too just like mobile. That page
becomes super long. Model the jobs page after mobile, but use the desktop's width to your
advantage where you can."

- The website shows one tab at a time on every screen: Details, Checklist, Findings, Photos and
  Wrap-Up (as each job type has them), as the phone's segmented control under the title. The tab
  is kept in the URL (`?tab=wrap-up`), so links, reloads and Back to the job return to it.
- Details keeps its two columns. On the widest screens (1280 px) the Checklist has the pool
  diagram, tests and findings beside it; Findings on its own puts the diagram beside the tests
  and findings; Photos uses four columns.
- Wrap-Up keeps the finish list on the left and the open step's editor in a panel on the right
  (from 1024 px), starting on the next step; Done puts it away. Phones and tablets use sheets.
- Phones are unchanged. The tab control is the job page's own segmented tab list (tab
  semantics for VoiceOver), styled as the `Segmented` control, not the form control itself.
