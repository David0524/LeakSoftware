# Gestures, browsing Today's card, and a faster Today

Owner, 2026-10-20 (after TestFlight 1010):

> Needs to be a better way to move the hero tile on Today back to a previous job. Also that page
> is super laggy as well. Everything needs to be fast, smooth, and clean. When I'm in a job I
> want to be able to swipe through the different pages. When I'm an owner on today, I want to
> swipe through the different tech's views. Add the gestures where they need to be throughout.
> Wait are there now 2 navigation buttons on the Hero Tile? Add a Live Activities toggle in the
> notifications setting.

One spec for the website (desktop and phone), the iPhone app and the Android app, planned with
the apple-design skill (`gestures.md`, `navigation-and-search.md`, `motion.md`,
`accessibility.md`, `live-activities.md`).

## 1. Today's card shows any stop

- **Swipe the card** left for the next stop and right for the one before. The card follows the
  finger, then settles on the stop with a spring (0.35 s); past the first or last stop it
  resists and springs back. A vertical drag still scrolls the page.
- **Tap a progress segment** to go to that stop. The bar keeps its 4 pt look, but each segment's
  tap area is 44 pt tall.
- While the card shows a stop the tech picked (`hero.browsing`), the figure's place on the
  right shows a **Back to Now** capsule (white at 18%, 15 pt semibold, 44 pt tall). It returns
  to the stop the day is on (`hero.currentId`). The card also goes back on its own when the app
  opens again, the day changes, or the tech starts or completes a stop.
- A finished stop shows as done: "Done · Stop 1 of 3", its name, Reopen Visit or Mark Not
  Complete where Today's row offers it, and no Start. A later stop shows as still ahead with its
  own Start, since a tech may take stops out of order.
- The rule lives in core: `todayHero({ ..., viewing })`. Only the card changes. The Live
  Activity, the ongoing notification and the Now row always follow the stop the day is on.
- Desktop website: the segments are buttons, and the Left and Right arrow keys change the stop
  while the card has focus.
- **VoiceOver and TalkBack:** the card is adjustable. Swipe up or down to change the stop,
  reading "Stop 2 of 3, Derek Jeter".
- **Reduce Motion:** the stop cross-fades instead of sliding.

## 2. One text button: On My Way lives in Message

The car (On My Way) sat beside the arrow (Directions) and read as a second navigation button.
Owner, 2026-10-20: "the message button should default to the 'on my way' text when you're
actually on your [way], but the tech can always edit the message. So let's just remove the car
icon and have the one text icon."

- The card's round buttons are **Message, Call, Directions**. There is no car.
- **Message** on the tech's own stop before they arrive (moments `ahead` and `driving`) opens
  the text already filled in with the On My Way message and arrival time (core
  `onMyWayText`, the words On My Way sends today).
  - The tech can edit it or clear it before sending.
  - Sending it records On My Way as the car did (`job.on_my_way_at`, `arrive_at`), so the card
    and the Live Activity switch to "On the way". The tech's edits are what's sent.
  - With the shop's number, it's the in-app compose sheet, sent through the existing On My Way
    path.
  - Without the shop's number, it's the phone's own Messages with the text filled in, recorded
    when opened, as the old fallback did.
- On site, after the stop, or when On My Way has already gone out today, Message opens a blank
  text, as before.
- An owner looking at a tech's day always gets a blank text.
- **Directions** and the address always open Maps directly.

## 3. Live Activity setting

- More › Notifications (website), Settings › Notifications (apps): a new switch, **Live
  Activity**, with the hint "Your current stop on the Lock Screen during the workday."
- It's on unless the person turns it off (`profile.notification_prefs.live`; core
  `NOTIFICATION_KINDS`).
- **Off:**
  - the iPhone ends its Live Activity and doesn't start one;
  - Android removes and doesn't post the ongoing notification;
  - the server sends no Live Activity updates to that person.
- Turning it back on starts it at once during the workday.

## 4. Swipe between pages

- **A job:** swipe left or right between the job's pages (the segmented control at the top).
  The control's selection follows the swipe. The pages keep their scroll position.
- **Today, for owners and admins:** swipe left or right between the techs' days. The Whose Day
  control follows. The order is the same as the control, and the swipe stops at either end.
- **Schedule, Day view:** swipe left or right for the day before or after, the same as its
  arrows.
- A swipe never starts on a map, a drag grip, the pool diagram, a signature pad or a horizontal
  scroller.
- Swiping is never the only way: the controls stay.
- Reduce Motion cross-fades instead of sliding.

## 5. Gestures throughout

- **Pull to refresh** on Today, Schedule, Jobs, Customers, Inbox and Quotes and Invoices, in the
  apps and on the phone website.
- **Edge swipe back** on every pushed screen. The iPhone's custom navigation bars must keep the
  system's interactive pop.
- **Swipe down** to dismiss sheets. A sheet with unsaved changes asks first.
- **Swipe actions on list rows**, always with a visible alternative on the row or its page:
  - Today's stops: leading Complete, trailing Call and Directions;
  - Inbox: Read or Unread;
  - customer, job and quote lists: trailing Call where there's a phone.
  - Destructive actions are never a full swipe.
- **Touch and hold** (context menu) on rows: Call, Message, Directions, Open.

## 6. Fast and smooth

- Today opens on cached data at once, then refreshes in place. No blank screen and no spinner
  over content.
- Browsing the card, changing techs and swiping pages are local state: no network round trip,
  and they respond within one frame.
- Work that isn't drawing stays off the main thread. Rows update only when their own data
  changes.
- Long lists are virtualized, and images and maps load lazily.
- Website: Today's queries run in parallel. Switching techs, browsing the card and swiping
  happen in the browser without a server round trip.
- A job page whose content fits on the screen doesn't scroll, so its large title stays put
  instead of shrinking onto a short tile (owner, 2026-10-20, of Findings: "it doesn't make sense
  for the nav bar to shrink on scroll since it's sitting on a tile"). The same holds for short
  Tests and Photos pages; a long page scrolls, and collapses the title, as usual.

## 7. Plan Week for several techs, split evenly

Owner, 2026-10-20: "You should be able to select multiple techs when planning a week. Then it
should split the open jobs up evenly."

- **Picking techs:** Plan Week's tech choice becomes a list with checkmarks.
  - An **Everyone** row at the top selects or clears all techs.
  - Each active tech has a row with their color dot and name.
  - At least one tech must be selected; with none, Plan Week is dimmed.
  - The control reads "2 Techs" or "Everyone" when closed.
- **Splitting the jobs:** with two or more techs, the open jobs are split evenly by minutes of
  work (core `planWeek({ ..., even: true })`).
  - Each job goes to a tech whose share is within one job of the lightest, then wherever it adds
    the least driving.
  - A job already given to one tech stays with that tech.
  - When no lighter tech has room, the job goes to any tech who does.
- **Result banner:** the first line is the usual summary, and the second line shows the split:
  "Ana 6 jobs · Ben 5 jobs · Cy 5 jobs" (core `planWeekSplit`).
- **Server:** `planWeek({ dates, techs: string[], tray })` replaces `tech`; "all" stays
  accepted, meaning every active tech.

## 8. Sorting Jobs, as Invoices sorts

Owner, 2026-10-20: "Add sorting on the jobs page just like invoices."

- Jobs gets the same Sort control as Invoices, in the same place, with the same behavior on each
  platform. It applies as soon as a sort is chosen and is kept in the page's address on the
  website.
- The options, in order, are Scheduled Date (the default, today's order), Newest, Oldest,
  Customer and Job Number (core `JOB_SORTS`, `jobSort`, `sortJobs`).
  - Scheduled Date: soonest first, jobs without a date last.
  - Newest and Oldest: by when the job was made.
  - Customer: A to Z, ignoring case and accents.
  - Job Number: highest first.
  - Ties go to the highest job number.
- The sort works together with the status filter and the search.

## 9. Today's flow, one stop at a time

Owner, 2026-10-20: "Make sure that navigation on the today page makes sense. I don't understand
when a job goes from marked completed to when it starts the next job."

**What was confusing.** After Mark Complete, the phones kept the finished stop on the card for a
minute ("Done · Derek Jeter"), but the card's Start button and On My Way were for the *next*
stop. Then the card jumped by itself. The website skipped that state and showed the next stop at
once. So one card spoke about two jobs, and the platforms behaved differently.

**The rule.** The card is always about one stop, and its buttons are for that stop. Every
change happens because the tech pressed something, never on a timer.

| The tech presses | The card shows | Top line | Buttons |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| (day starts) | Stop 1 | Up next · Stop 1 of 3 | Start, Message (On My Way text), Call, Directions |
| Message › Send | Stop 1 | On the way · Stop 1 of 3 | Start, Message, Call, Directions |
| Start | Stop 1 | At the pool · Stop 1 of 3 | Open Checklist, Mark Complete |
| Mark Complete | Stop 2 (slides in from the right) | Up next · Stop 2 of 3 | Start, Message (On My Way text), … |
| Mark Complete on the last stop | The day | All 3 stops done | none |

- **Mark Complete moves the card on at once.**
  - The finished stop's segment fills, its checkmark draws on the card for a moment (0.6 s;
    none with Reduce Motion), and the card slides to the next stop, the same slide as swiping.
  - The top banner says "Derek Jeter done" with **Undo** (Reopen Visit or Mark Not Complete,
    whichever Today's row offers). It goes away by itself, as the other result banners do.
  - Nothing waits for a minute. The phones' `justDone` hold is removed: apps stop passing
    `justDone` to `todayHero`.
- **A finished stop**, reached by swiping back, is about that stop only. Its top line is "Done ·
  Stop 1 of 3", and its one button is Reopen Visit or Mark Not Complete. It never has Start for
  another stop (core `heroActions("done")` now returns `{ primary: null, undo: true }` for the
  tech).
- **Completing from the job page** (Complete Job) works the same. Back on Today, the card shows
  the next stop and the banner offers Undo.
- **Lock Screen:** after Mark Complete, the Live Activity and the Android notification go
  straight to the next stop ("Up next: Wayne Gretzky at 9:15 AM"). The iPhone update carries an
  alert, "Derek Jeter done. Next: Wayne Gretzky", so the change is noticed.

**Getting around Today:**
- The card is where you are. Swipe it or tap a segment to look at another stop, and use Back to
  Now to return.
- The list below is the whole day:
  - tapping a row opens that job;
  - the card's stop is the row marked Now;
  - a finished row shows a checkmark.
- Tapping the customer's name on the card opens the job.
- Coming back to Today keeps the scroll position and the card's stop.

## 10. No clock times on the Live Activity or the card

Owner, 2026-10-20: "I don't like times in the live activity. Pool Jobs aren't really scheduled for
a certain time. A window of anything. The times are more for the tech's own sake than anything,
but it's mostly irrelevant."

- **Before arriving** (Up next, On the way): the figure is the stop's place in the day, "2 of 5",
  with "Stop" under it. The compact Dynamic Island shows "2/5".
- **At the pool:** the figure stays the checklist, "4 of 9". Once done, it's the checkmark.
- **The next stop** is named without a time: "Next: Wayne Gretzky".
- **Spoken text** has no times either.
- **The card's top line** names the stop's place only once (core `heroTopLine`):
  - "Up next" when the figure already shows "2 of 5";
  - "At the pool · Stop 2 of 5" on site.
- **Unchanged:**
  - Today's list keeps each stop's time as a small detail for the tech.
  - The On My Way text to the customer keeps its arrival estimate.

## 11. The first download: a pool that fills

Owner, 2026-10-20: "Downloading your company takes a long time. Give a more interesting loading
bar." This is the apps only; the website has no download.

**Real progress.**
- Before the first pull, the app asks the server how much there is: `public.sync_pull_count()`,
  rows per table, counted with the person's own access (migration 7700).
- As pages arrive, the sync engine counts rows per table. Core `downloadProgress(pulled,
  totals)` turns that into one fraction and a few kinds the shop knows: Settings, Customers,
  Pools, Jobs, Photos and Videos, Reports, Quotes, and Invoices, and Messages. Only kinds the
  shop has are listed.
- If the count fails (an older server, or offline at that moment), the screen still works: the
  water moves with no percent.

**Faster.** The first download asks for 2,000 rows a page, the most `sync_pull` allows, instead
of 500. While one page is being saved, the next is already on its way.

**The screen ("Getting Ready").**
- **The pool:** a rounded pool outline, about 220 by 120 pt, in `color.controlBorder`. Water in
  `color.brand.water` fills from the bottom to the fraction, with a slow wave on its surface (a
  2 s loop). The level eases to each new value over 0.4 s. Under Reduce Motion the surface is
  flat and the level steps.
- **The percent:** under the pool, "42%" in Barlow ExtraBold 34 with tabular figures. The
  number rolls as it changes. Under it, "Downloading your company" in 17 pt.
- **The kinds:** an inset grouped list, one row per kind: its symbol, its label, and "312 of
  1,240" in tabular figures on the right. The row being downloaded shows a small spinner, and a
  finished row shows a checkmark in `color.link`.
- **Footer:** "This happens once. After this, the app works without signal."
- **Finishing:** the water reaches the top, the percent becomes a checkmark for 0.6 s, then the
  app opens.
- **Offline, an error, and Try Again** stay as they are now.
- **VoiceOver and TalkBack:** the pool is one progress element whose value is
  `downloadProgress().spoken`. Each kind row reads "Jobs, 312 of 1,240", or "Jobs, done".
