# Undo, no early warnings, and a tap on completion

Owner, 2026-10-26: "Do 1, 2, 5" of the Apple-principles list. All three ship on the website
(desktop and phone), the iPhone app and the Android app with the same wording and behavior.

## 1. Undo instead of "Are you sure?"

Apple's rule: an action that can be taken back doesn't ask first; it happens, and a short
message offers Undo. An action that can't be taken back, or that other people see, still asks.

### How it works

A removal on a visit is already a soft delete (`deleted_at`), so it is reversible everywhere:

- The removal runs at once, through the same path as before (website: server action; phones:
  row and outbox in one transaction).
- A banner at the top of the screen says what happened and offers Undo for **five seconds**:
  "Photo removed · Undo". It is the banner each platform already uses for results (website
  `top-banner.tsx`, iPhone `ResultBanner.swift`, Android `ResultBanner.tsx`), not a snackbar. A
  screen reader gets it read aloud and a longer time on the iPhone.
- Undo writes `deleted_at: null` on the same rows, the same way (website: `restore*` server
  actions; phones: the sync layer), so the row, its id and every other column come back exactly.
- A removal made from a sheet or a pushed screen is announced on the screen that is showing
  once it closes (iPhone `RemovalCenter`, Android `RemovedHost` in the signed-in layout).
- Where the old question carried a fact, the banner keeps it as its second line: a scheduled
  cleaning removed from a checklist says "It's still due, so it goes on the next visit."; a
  repair says "It comes off the job's price. An invoice already made keeps it."

Shared wording and timing live in `packages/core/src/removal.ts` (`REMOVED_TEXT`,
`REMOVED_UNDO_LABEL`, `REMOVED_UNDO_SECONDS`), ported to `SundeckCore/Removal.swift` and checked by
the `removal` fixtures. Menus and swipe actions for a converted row say "Delete" or "Remove", no
longer "Delete…" (the ellipsis meant a question follows).

## 2. No warnings before the person acts

Nothing says "you can't do this yet" until the person tries to do it.

- **Complete Job with "Take a pool photo first" on.** The phone job page, Today and the website
  already waited for the tap. Now the explanation comes with the way out at that moment:
  "Take a photo of the pool before finishing this job." with **Take Photo** (the website's top
  banner has the button, which opens the job's Photos).
- **Review and Sign** (Android) showed the same red notice with its buttons turned off the
  moment the screen opened. It now shows nothing until Finish or Sign and Finish is tapped
  without a photo, then explains with Take Photo, as the iPhone's sign-off already did.
- **"Add a photo to send this report."** (Require a Photo) sits where Send Visit Report would
  be and replaces its buttons, so it stays, but as plain text with Add Photo, not a red bar.
- Left as they are, on purpose: sync and offline status (the sync bar, "You're offline"),
  setup notices that replace a screen (no company number, payments not set up, email not set
  up), a checklist that still has items to do where the signature pad would be, and "Check these
  before sending" on a written report draft, which is advice about the draft's content while it
  is being edited. That last one is the closest call; if the owner wants it only at send time,
  it moves to the Send buttons on all four platforms.

## 5. A tap on completion

A tap you can feel at the moments something finishes. The moments are listed once, in
`packages/core/src/haptics.ts` (`HAPTIC_MOMENTS`), ported to `SundeckCore/Haptics.swift` and
checked by the same fixtures:

| Moment | Strength | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A checklist item marked done ("Done", "Not Leaking", "No") | light | `ChecklistTab` (iPhone), `ChecklistTab` and `AnswerControls` (Android), the checklist row (website) |
| A chemical marked added | light | `ChemicalCard`, `VisitChemicals`, `visit-chemicals.tsx` |
| Mark All Added | success | same |
| A job completed | success | job page, Today and the Jobs list on every platform |
| A report sent (visit report, or the finished report by email or text) | success | `ReportTab` and `VisitReportViews`, `ReportTab` and `VisitReport`, `report-panel.tsx` and `visit-report-panel.tsx` |
| A payment recorded | success | `InvoiceSection` and `InvoicesScreen`, `Receipt.tsx`, `payment-receipt.tsx` |

- iPhone: `.sensoryFeedback` through `View.haptic(_:trigger:)` (`UI/Haptics.swift`), a light
  impact or the success feedback. The app already targets iOS 18.
- Android: `expo-haptics` (already a dependency) through `haptic(moment)` in `ui/haptics.ts`.
- Website: `navigator.vibrate(10)` through `buzz(moment)` in `lib/haptics.ts`, only where the
  browser has it (Android browsers; iPhone Safari does nothing) and never with Reduce Motion.

Not buzzed on purpose: answers to a pool inspection's questions (they are choices, not "done"),
and Undo itself.
