# Design review: Tidemark, full pre-pilot pass (M11)

Reviewed with the Apple Design skill (Apple Human Interface Guidelines). Files read for this
review: `accessibility.md`, `layout.md`, `typography.md`, `color.md`, `designing-for-ios.md`,
`cross-platform.md`, plus `tab-bars.md`, `buttons.md`, `modality.md`, `sheets.md`,
`entering-data.md`, `text-fields.md`, `lists-and-tables.md`, `feedback.md`, `writing.md`,
`dark-mode.md`, `motion.md`. Citations are `file.md › Heading`; anything else is marked as
judgment.

**Website (measured).** `apps/web` production build (`next start`) against the hosted dev
project, Chromium via playwright-core, read-only (nothing was sent, signed, saved or deleted).
Viewports: phone 390x844 (touch, 2x) and desktop 1280x900, light and dark, plus a 320 px reflow
pass. Signed in as the demo owner and the demo tech. Screens: `/`, `/sign-in`, `/sign-up`,
`/today` (and `?tech=` chips), `/jobs` (and `?status=` filter), job #1038 with each phone part
(Details, Checklist, Findings, Photos, Wrap-Up), `/jobs/new` (Leak Detection and Maintenance),
`/schedule` (day, week, month, `?tech=all`), `/customers` and one customer, `/invoices`,
`/dashboard`, `/warranties`, `/more`, every `/settings/*` page, a customer report `/r/<token>`
and quote `/q/<token>`. `/sign-off/<id>` was not opened: no link to it is rendered on the job
page, and opening a sign-off session by guessing an id is not read-only browsing.

An injected script checked every visible interactive element and text node on each screen:
WCAG 2 contrast from computed colors (backgrounds composited up the tree, `color(srgb …)` glass
fills included), hit boxes under 44x44 at phone width, accessible names, input labels, heading
order, `scrollWidth` vs `clientWidth`, and text under 13 px. Keyboard focus was checked by
tabbing 40 stops through a job page on desktop. Token contrast was computed from
`brand/tokens.json`.

**Field app (code reading only).** `apps/mobile` cannot run here. Findings marked *(code)* come
from reading `src/app/**`, `src/features/**`, `src/ui/components.tsx` and `src/ui/theme.ts`;
they were not seen on a device.

Note: one run hit "This page couldn't load" on `/warranties`; that was the coordinator rebuilding
`.next` under the running server. After a restart the page loaded normally. Not a finding.

## Summary

Tidemark's job is to get a leak tech through an inspection and to the money without paperwork,
and to give the office the same picture. It does that with a calm, trade-appropriate system:
navy header, soft cards, pill controls, a glass tab bar on phones, and the pool diagram with
numbered severity pins as its signature. Dark mode is clean (the automated pass found **zero**
contrast failures in dark on every screen), focus rings show on every stop, and the icon buttons
carry real names ("Call Sam Sample, (480) 555-0123").

**Overall rating: Critical issues.** Three things block the pilot: the public home's
**Sign In button is navy text on the navy hero (1.00:1)** in light mode; several standalone links
on phone are **below Apple's 28 pt minimum** (17 to 26 px tall); and on Severities **four of five
hex color fields have no label** because every card reuses `id="color"`. Close behind: the link
blue sits at 4.49:1 on the page background, the route warning text at 4.29:1, and web text sizes
are in `px`, so they ignore the person's browser text size.

Counts: 3 Critical, 4 High, 11 Medium, 10 Low.

## Critical

### C1. Home hero "Sign In" button is invisible in light mode

- **Screen:** `/` (public home), phone and desktop, light.
- **Code:** `apps/web/src/app/(public)/page.tsx:50-56`, `apps/web/src/components/ui.tsx:14-31`.
- **What:** the secondary button gets `text-text bg-surface` from `BUTTON.secondary`, and the hero
  passes `bg-transparent text-on-header` in `className`. Both are single-property utilities, so
  which one wins depends on Tailwind's CSS order, not on the order in the class string. Measured:
  text `rgb(18,38,58)` on the navy hero `rgb(18,38,58)`, **1.00:1**. The screenshot shows an empty
  outlined pill beside "Start Free Trial". In dark mode `dark:text-on-header` wins, so it reads.
- **Why:** `accessibility.md › Vision`: "Strive to meet color contrast minimum standards"
  (4.5:1 at this size). `buttons.md › Content`: "Ensure that each button clearly communicates its
  purpose."
- **Fix:** do not override colors through `className`. Add a kind for dark surfaces:
  `onDark: "border border-on-header text-on-header hover:bg-white/10"` in `BUTTON`, then
  `<ButtonLink href="/sign-in" kind="onDark">Sign In</ButtonLink>`. White on navy is 15.4:1.
  Optionally add `tailwind-merge` to `cx()` so a later override always wins. Add a Playwright
  assertion on the hero link's computed color so this cannot regress.

### C2. Standalone links under the 28 pt minimum on phone

- **Screens and measured hit boxes (390 px wide):**
  - `/dashboard` "Open invoices": **97x17** (`app/(office)/dashboard/page.tsx:64`).
  - Job Wrap-Up "Set up payments": **135x20** (`app/(office)/jobs/[id]/page.tsx:720`).
  - `/sign-in` "Start a free trial": **126x20** (`app/(public)/sign-in/page.tsx:19`).
  - `/schedule?view=week` day headings ("Monday, October 12"): **139x22**
    (`app/(office)/schedule/page.tsx:352`).
  - Job page customer name "Sam Sample": **119x24** (`app/(office)/jobs/[id]/page.tsx:130`).
  - `/today` stop cards, customer/job link: **330x26** (`components/route-board.tsx`,
    `StopCard`, around line 448); schedule day "Needs a Time" rows "Kevin Morris · #1089":
    **262x26** (`route-board.tsx:607`).
  - Customer page "Job #1039" / "Job #1038": **81x26** (`app/(office)/customers/[id]/page.tsx:126`).
  - Dashboard "Jobs by Status" rows: 318x**34** (`dashboard/page.tsx:137`), above the minimum
    but under the default.
- **Why:** `accessibility.md › Mobility`: iOS "Default control size 44x44 pt, Minimum control size
  28x28 pt". These are not links inside a sentence; they are the only way to reach the target,
  used one-handed in a truck.
- **Fix:** give every standalone link the same box as buttons. In `ui.tsx` export
  `const LINK = "inline-flex min-h-11 items-center text-link underline-offset-4 hover:underline"`
  and use it for the links above. For the stacked name/time links in `StopCard` and the day rows,
  make the whole row the link: `className="block min-h-11 py-2 …"`. Dashboard rows:
  `py-1` to `min-h-11 py-2`. Week headings: wrap the `<h2>` link in `inline-flex min-h-11`.

### C3. Severity color fields have no label (duplicate `id="color"`)

- **Screen:** `/settings/severities`.
- **Code:** `apps/web/src/components/settings-forms.tsx:124-144` (`ColorField`), called from
  `app/(office)/settings/severities/page.tsx:24` once per severity plus the Add form.
- **What:** `ColorField` uses `id={name}`, so all five text inputs are `id="color"`. Each
  `<label htmlFor="color">` resolves to the first one; the other **4 inputs have no accessible
  name** (measured: `input.labels` empty, no `aria-label`). VoiceOver reads "edit text, #C62F2B".
  The same pattern in `Field`/`Select` (`id = input.id ?? input.name`) is safe only because the
  price book and team pages pass unique ids.
- **Why:** `accessibility.md › Vision`: describe the interface "for VoiceOver";
  `writing.md › Best practices`: "label all fields clearly".
- **Fix:** `const id = useId();` in `ColorField`, then `<label htmlFor={id}>` and
  `<input id={id} …>`, and pass the label to the swatch picker as `aria-label={`${label} picker`}`
  (already done). Do the same in `Field`, `TextArea` and `Select` so a missing `id` can never
  collide: `const auto = useId(); const id = input.id ?? auto;` (these are server-safe if moved
  to a client file, or derive the id from a required `id` prop).

## Improvements

### H1. High: link blue fails 4.5:1 on the page background (4.49:1)

- **Screens:** every back link ("← Jobs", "← Customers"), job page "Show 39 More", Wrap-Up
  "Set up payments", the week view "Wednesday, October 14 · Today" heading, `/today` stop number
  (`text-bg` on `bg-link`).
- **What:** `role.light.link` `#1F6FAE` on `role.light.bg` `#E9ECEF` = **4.49:1** at 17 px regular.
  On white cards it is 5.33:1, which is why most links pass; the failures are the ones placed on
  the page background. The field app uses the same token on `color.bg` *(code)*.
- **Why:** `accessibility.md › Vision`: text up to 17 pt needs 4.5:1.
  `color.md › Inclusive color`: "Avoid using colors that make it hard to perceive content."
- **Fix:** in `brand/tokens.json` set `role.light.link` and `role.light.focus` to `#1A63A0`
  (5.31:1 on `#E9ECEF`, 6.29:1 on white; white on it 6.29:1), run `pnpm tokens`. Keep
  `brand.water` for the diagram and charts. Bold 17 px stop numbers pass Apple's 3:1 bold rule
  already, but they follow the token and improve too.

### H2. High: route warnings at 4.29:1, and "13 min short" is unclear

- **Screen:** `/today` and `/today?tech=…` (owner and tech).
- **Code:** `apps/web/src/components/route-board.tsx:392-397` (`Leg`).
- **What:** "· 13 min short" is 15 px semibold `#9A6210` on the page background `#E9ECEF`:
  **4.29:1**. The words also leave the tech to work out that the drive is longer than the gap, so
  the next stop will start late.
- **Why:** `accessibility.md › Vision` (4.5:1 under 18 pt). `writing.md › Best practices`:
  "Be clear… If you can use fewer words, do so", and errors should say what someone can do.
- **Fix:** darken `status.warning` to `#8A5A0E` (4.99:1 on `#E9ECEF`, 5.92:1 on white, white on
  it 5.92:1; it also serves the "Quote accepted" chip). Reword to
  `` `· ${durationLabel(short)} late` `` and give the line `title`/`aria-label`
  "Arrives 13 min after the scheduled time".

### H3. High: web text ignores the person's text size setting

- **Screens:** all. **Code:** `apps/web/src/app/globals.css:12` (`font-size: 17px`), 149 uses of
  `text-[15px]`, 15 of `text-[13px]`, 2 of `text-[11px]` (tab labels), 9 of `text-[17px]`.
- **What:** body and secondary text are fixed in `px`, while Tailwind's `text-xl`/`text-3xl`
  headings are in `rem`. Page zoom still works (reflow at 320 px is fine on most pages, see M1),
  but a person who raises the browser's default font size gets bigger headings and the same body
  text: the hierarchy inverts and the content they wanted bigger stays put.
- **Why:** `accessibility.md › Vision`: "Support larger text sizes… give people the option to
  enlarge text by at least 200 percent." `typography.md › Supporting Dynamic Type`: "Make sure
  your app's layout adapts to all font sizes."
- **Fix:** set the scale in `rem` from the default 16 px: `body { font-size: 1.0625rem }`
  (17 px), add theme sizes in `globals.css`: `@theme { --text-body: 1.0625rem; --text-secondary:
  0.9375rem; --text-caption: 0.8125rem; --text-tab: 0.6875rem }` and replace `text-[17px]`,
  `text-[15px]`, `text-[13px]`, `text-[11px]` with `text-body`, `text-secondary`,
  `text-caption`, `text-tab` (a codemod is safe: same pixel result at default settings).

### H4. High: field app shrinks or truncates text at large Dynamic Type *(code)*

- **Code:** `apps/mobile/src/ui/components.tsx:262-268` (`Segmented`: `numberOfLines={1}
  adjustsFontSizeToFit`), `apps/mobile/src/app/(app)/index.tsx` job rows (`numberOfLines={1}` on
  customer and address), fixed `lineHeight` on every `T` variant (`components.tsx:44-58`).
  Only `features/media/MediaSection.tsx:54` adapts layout to `fontScale`.
- **What:** with five job sections ("Checklist / Tests / Findings / Report / Quote") at 15 pt in
  one row, larger text makes `adjustsFontSizeToFit` scale the labels back down, which cancels the
  person's setting exactly where they navigate. Job list rows cut the customer and address to one
  line, so at AX sizes a tech sees "Grace Hol…" and "7120 E Cac…".
- **Why:** `typography.md › Supporting Dynamic Type`: "Keep text truncation to a minimum as font
  size increases" and "Consider adjusting your layout at large font sizes". `layout.md ›
  Adaptability`: "horizontally adjacent views may need to stack vertically".
- **Fix:** in `Segmented`, read `const { fontScale } = useWindowDimensions();` and when
  `fontScale >= 1.3` render the options as a `Choice` (wrapping pills) instead of one row; drop
  `adjustsFontSizeToFit`. In the job list use `numberOfLines={fontScale >= 1.3 ? undefined : 1}`.
  Scale line heights with the text: `lineHeight: 22 * Math.min(fontScale, 2)` or remove fixed
  `lineHeight` on single-line labels. Test with Settings › Accessibility › Larger Text at AX3.

### M1. Medium: schedule week and month scroll sideways at 320 px

- **Screen:** `/schedule?view=week` and `?view=month`, 320 px wide (iPhone SE size, or 400% zoom
  of a 1280 px window). Measured `scrollWidth` **341** vs `clientWidth` 320; the fixed tab bar
  stretches to the 341 px layout and its right edge goes off screen. Month cells are 35x64 at
  this width. All other screens reflow at 320 px.
- **Why:** `layout.md › Adaptability`: "Design a layout that adapts gracefully and consistently."
- **Fix:** find the 341 px child (likely the date row: `‹` + date field + `›` + segmented track
  with `whitespace-nowrap`) and let it wrap: `flex flex-wrap gap-2` on the row and `min-w-0` on
  the date field. Add `overflow-x-clip` to the page wrapper only after the cause is fixed.

### M2. Medium: long emails run under the Role menu on Team

- **Screen:** `/settings/team`, phone, light and dark. The e2e tech's address
  ("e2e-tech-…@demo.leakbook.test") overlaps the Role `<select>`.
- **Code:** `app/(office)/settings/team/page.tsx:117-124`.
- **Why:** `typography.md › Supporting Dynamic Type`: inline items "can crowd text and cause
  truncation or overlapping".
- **Fix:** `<div className="min-w-0 flex-1 basis-48">` and

### M3. Medium: Edit and Delete are small text links side by side

- **Screens:** job Findings (tests and findings), Photos ("Mark Up" / "Delete").
- **Code:** `components/job-work.tsx:550-570` and around 831; `components/media-work.tsx:445-468`.
- **What:** "Edit" measures **32x44**; Delete sits 16 px away in red text. Each Delete asks
  `window.confirm`, which helps, but these are the only buttons on the site that are not pills.
- **Why:** `accessibility.md › Mobility`: "Consider spacing between controls as important as
  size… about 24 points of padding" for elements without a bezel. House rule: buttons are pills.
- **Fix:** use the shared buttons: `<Button kind="secondary" className="min-w-24">Edit</Button>`
  and `<Button kind="danger">Delete</Button>` in a `flex gap-3` row, or move Delete into the edit
  form so it is one step further from a stray tap.

### M4. Medium: the glass section switcher lets body text show through

- **Screen:** job page on phone, while scrolling (Wrap-Up and Findings especially).
- **Code:** `components/job-sections.tsx:42` (`glass sticky …`), `app/globals.css` `.glass`
  (`surface 72%`, blur 24 px).
- **What:** 17 px body text and underlined links scroll under "Details / Checklist / Findings /
  Photos / Wrap-Up" and stay readable through the bar, competing with the labels.
- **Why:** `color.md › Liquid Glass color`: "make sure its default or resting state… maintains
  clear legibility"; `layout.md › Visual hierarchy`: use a scroll edge effect to elevate controls.
- **Fix:** raise the mix to 86% for the switcher (`.glass-strong { background: color-mix(in srgb,
  var(--lb-surface) 86%, transparent) }`) and add a soft scroll edge under it:
  `after:pointer-events-none after:absolute after:inset-x-0 after:-bottom-3 after:h-3
  after:bg-gradient-to-b after:from-bg after:to-transparent`.

### M5. Medium: "Cancel Job" is the first control on the job page

- **Screen:** job page, phone and desktop, owner and tech.
- **Code:** `components/job-work.tsx:81-107` (status actions), rendered at the top of
  `app/(office)/jobs/[id]/page.tsx`.
- **What:** a red outlined pill sits directly under the title, above the customer and the section
  switcher, on every open job. It confirms, but it is still the most prominent action on a page
  whose job is doing the work.
- **Why:** `buttons.md › Role`: "Don't assign the primary role to a button that performs a
  destructive action"; `designing-for-ios.md › Best practices`: limit onscreen controls and make
  secondary actions "discoverable with minimal interaction".
- **Fix:** move Cancel Job to the end of the Details part (or a `<details>` "More actions"
  disclosure there) and keep the status chip alone under the title.

### M6. Medium: pin placement on the pool diagram is invisible to VoiceOver *(code)*

- **Code:** `apps/mobile/src/ui/PoolDiagram.tsx:44-52`.
- **What:** the label sits on a plain `View` without `accessible`, so iOS ignores it; the
  `Pressable` that places the pin has no role or label. A VoiceOver user can type a location in
  the finding's text field but cannot place or hear the pins.
- **Why:** `accessibility.md › Mobility`: "Offer alternatives to gestures"; `accessibility.md ›
  Vision`: describe the interface for VoiceOver.
- **Fix:** on the `Pressable`: `accessibilityRole="adjustable"` or `"button"`,
  `accessibilityLabel="Pool diagram"`, `accessibilityHint="Double-tap to place the pin in the
  middle, then use the location field to describe it"`, and an `onAccessibilityTap` that places
  the pin at a default spot. Give each pin a summary in the parent: `accessibilityValue={{ text:
  pins.map(p => `Pin ${p.label}`).join(", ") }}`.

### M7. Medium: Jobs and Checklist Components render everything at once

- **Screens:** `/jobs` on phone is about **37,000 px** tall (every job, no paging, no search);

- **Why:** `layout.md › Visual hierarchy`: "Use progressive disclosure to make layouts cleaner";
  `lists-and-tables.md › Content`: keep rows succinct.
- **Fix:** Jobs: render the first 50 and a `Show More` link (`?limit=`), and add the same search

### M8. Medium: no skip link; 11 Tab stops before the content

- **Screen:** every office page on desktop. Tab order measured on the job page: logo, 8 nav links,

- **Why:** `accessibility.md › Speech`: "Let people use the keyboard alone to navigate and interact

### M9. Medium: field app test result chip is 12 pt and square *(code)*

- **Code:** `apps/mobile/src/features/inspection/OutcomeChip.tsx:23-26`.
- **What:** "PASS / FAIL / UNCLEAR" at 12 pt uppercase with `radius.sm` (3). Every other chip in

- **Why:** `typography.md › Ensuring legibility` (minimum sizes, avoid tiny caps); house rule:

### M10. Medium: Sign Out sits in the phone header on every screen

### M11. Medium: list rows have no pressed state on touch

## Craft notes

- **Point of view (keep):** the pool diagram with numbered severity pins, carried from the phone
  to the office page and into the customer report, is the one thing this product will be
  remembered by. The "10 Items Pass / 1 Needs Attention / 1 Leaking" summary tiles on the report
  use number, word and color together, which is the right way to do status.
- **Restraint is mostly there.** Orange now appears once per screen (New Job, Create Invite,
  Create Job, Start Free Trial); the M6 "orange on every Save" finding is fixed. The one exception
  is the public header plus hero on `/` at desktop, both orange "Start Free Trial".
- **Medium: the Today stop card puts actions before identity.** Call and On My Way sit on their
  own row above the customer's name, leaving an empty band at the left. Put the name and address
  first, then the action row (`route-board.tsx` StopCard). (`layout.md › Visual hierarchy`: "place
  the most important items near the top and leading side".)
- **Typography** carries the brand well: Barlow Semi Condensed for titles and buttons, Public Sans
  for reading. The uppercase tracked section labels ("CUSTOMER", "POOL") at 13 px are the only
  place the type gets small; they pass contrast (4.82:1 on bg, 5.72:1 on white).
- **Remove one accessory:** the dotted leader line next to each drive leg on Today adds nothing
  the text does not say.

## What works

- **Focus:** every one of 40 Tab stops on the job page draws a 3 px outline (white in the navy
  header, focus blue elsewhere, red on Cancel Job). Keep `:focus-visible` in `globals.css`.
- **Dark mode:** zero automated contrast failures across 30+ screens in dark, desktop and phone.
  The status chips flagged in M6 now use `text-on-status` and pass in both appearances.
- **Names:** every icon-only control found has a spoken name with context ("Call Sam Sample,
  (480) 555-0123", "Text Sam Sample", "Delete Pressure test", "Drag Kevin Morris"). The drag
  handles on the schedule all have a "Move…" button alternative.
- **Structure:** every page has `lang="en"`, a `<main>`, a unique title, one `h1`, and headings in
  order; the job sections are a real `tablist`/`tabpanel`; nav links set `aria-current="page"`.
- **Touch:** pills, segments, chips, icon buttons and fields are 44 px or more everywhere they use
  the shared classes (`buttonClass`, `pillClass`, `segmentClass`, `iconButtonClass`, `INPUT`).
  The field app's buttons and fields are 52 pt (`touch + 4`), segments 44 pt, swatches 44 pt hit
  areas around 32 pt dots *(code)*.
- **Glass done responsibly:** only the header, tab bar and section switcher use it, and
  `prefers-reduced-transparency` / `prefers-contrast: more` switch them to solid fills and give
  cards an outline.
- **Reflow:** 8 of 10 screens tested at 320 px have no sideways scroll.
- **Field app alerts** pair every destructive button with a named way out ("Keep", "Keep
  Editing", "Keep Job", "Stay Signed In"), and the sync bar states problems in words, not only
  with its colored dot *(code)*.

## Platform notes

- **Web, not native.** HIG platform conventions apply as principles. The phone layout follows the
  iOS pattern correctly: a 5-tab bottom bar with single-word labels and filled-style icons, the
  section switcher as a segmented control, and a More tab for Invoices, Dashboard, Warranties and
  Settings. `tab-bars.md › Best practices` warns against overflow tabs; with eight destinations a
  More tab is the reasonable trade, and it should stay the last tab.
- **Appearance switch.** The site has its own sun/moon toggle and does not follow the device
  (owner, 2026-10-13). `dark-mode.md › Best practices`: "Avoid offering an app-specific appearance
  setting." Recommendation for the owner, not a finding: keep the toggle but use the device
  setting until someone presses it (in `THEME_SCRIPT`, fall back to
  `matchMedia("(prefers-color-scheme: dark)")` when nothing is stored).
- **Field app is light only** (`app.json` `userInterfaceStyle: "light"`), a recorded choice for
  sunlight; the tokens already have a dark set if that changes.
- **Customer documents** (`/r`, `/q`) stay light regardless of the toggle; right for documents
  that carry the shop's brand and get printed.
- **Not covered:** `/sign-off/<id>`, `/i/<token>` invoices, `/stop/<token>`, `/invite/<token>`,
  `/setup`, and any field-app screen on a device. The field app findings need a check on an
  iPhone at AX3 text size with VoiceOver before the pilot.
